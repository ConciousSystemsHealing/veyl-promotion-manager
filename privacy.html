Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

VEYL Promotion Manager is a personal desktop application under development for organizing and publishing promotional media.

Information accessed

When the operator authorizes a supported social media account, the application may request account identification and video publishing permissions needed for its functionality.

Use of information

Authorized information is used to connect the selected account, prepare publishing operations, and record publishing results.

Local storage

The application is designed to run on the operator's computer. Account authorization credentials and publishing history may be stored locally. The operator is responsible for protecting access to that computer.

Sharing

The application is not intended to sell personal information or provide account data to advertisers. Information necessary to publish content is transmitted to the authorized social media platform.

Retention and removal

The operator can remove locally stored application data and revoke account access through the relevant platform's account settings. Removing local data does not automatically remove videos already published on a platform.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application's features and integrations develop.
